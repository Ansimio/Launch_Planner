## Explicit assumptions
£1,000 budget; £28 selling price; £2.50 customer shipping; 15 orders; 20-print batch; £6 printer delivery per purchase; £0.60 packaging set bought in lots of 25; £0 packaging delivery (unverified); £80 preparation and £120 campaign costs; 3% receipt reserve (not a measured return rate); 4 hours preparation and 10 minutes per order valued at £15/hour; five calendar-day printer lead-time assumption.

No demand, conversion, customer acquisition cost, ROI or customer-satisfaction benchmark is invented. No marketplace commission because the scenario uses direct sales. Income tax, ongoing overhead, payment timing, stock impairment and salvage value are excluded. Inventory is valued at input cost. The model is a bounded decision exercise, not net-profit forecasting.
